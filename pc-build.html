What happens next

One survey. One coherent build.

  • We translate your games, apps, resolution, performance goals, and aesthetics into hardware priorities.
  • When verified automated research is available, current parts are researched from reputable US retailers, with Amazon and Newegg prioritized where practical.
  • Before an automated price is shown, the exact core configuration must pass Newegg PC Builder compatibility verification and manufacturer-specification checks for sockets, memory type, physical clearances, cooling, storage interfaces, BIOS support, PSU connectors, and power headroom.
  • PCPartPicker is used as an additional compatibility and parts-list cross-check when it is available. Newegg PC Builder and manufacturer specifications remain the required authoritative checks, so a temporary PCPartPicker outage does not create a fake incompatibility result.
  • If automated research is unavailable, or if the Newegg PC Builder check or any required manufacturer compatibility fact cannot be verified, the saved request is held for staff review and no automated estimate is released.
  • If compatibility is verified, you may see the recommended components and one estimated total—not GotCracked’s internal part-cost breakdown.
  • The sourcing trail and internal pricing remain with your GotCracked build request for staff review and purchasing.
