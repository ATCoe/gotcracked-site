Ask about a repair
How long will my repair take?+

Many common repairs can be completed the same day. Timing depends on diagnosis, device model, parts availability, and your approval. We confirm the expected turnaround before work begins.

Will I know the price before you start?+

Yes. After diagnosis, you receive a clear estimate. Work begins only after you approve it.

What should I do before bringing in my device?+

Back up important data when possible and bring any accessories related to the problem. Do not submit passwords or passcodes through this website.

Do repairs include a warranty?+

Eligible repairs include a 90-day workmanship warranty. Your final service record identifies the coverage for your specific repair.

What kinds of devices do you repair?+

We service many phones, tablets, computers, game consoles, and wearables. Submit your model and issue and we will confirm service availability.
