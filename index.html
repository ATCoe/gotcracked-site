A few things you might be wondering.

Have a different question? Call us or .

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome. If you have a time in mind, request an appointment and we'll confirm your visit. For mail-in repairs, wait for approval before shipping.

How much will my repair cost?

It depends on your device and the problem. Diagnostics are free, and we'll explain your estimate before starting billable repair work.

Can you fix it the same day?

Many common repairs can be completed the same day. We'll confirm timing after checking your device and parts availability.

Is my repair covered by a warranty?

Eligible repairs include a six-month workmanship warranty. Your service record will explain the coverage for your repair.

What should I bring?

Bring your device and any charger or accessory related to the issue. Back up important files if you can. Please don't send passwords or passcodes through the website.
