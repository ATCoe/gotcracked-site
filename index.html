Before You Bring It In

A Few Good
Questions.

Need another answer?
Call Our Shop →

Do I Need an Appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome. You can also request a visit online. A requested window is confirmed by our team; it is not a guaranteed repair completion time.

How Much Will My Repair Cost?

Diagnostics are free. Repair pricing depends on your device, the issue and the parts needed. We explain the estimate before beginning billable repair work.

Can You Repair It the Same Day?

Some repairs can be completed the same day. We confirm timing after evaluating the device and checking parts availability.

How Does Student Pickup Work?

Request pickup with your device details and a suggested meeting spot. We confirm eligibility, location and timing. Transport is free for approved student pickups; repair work is quoted separately.

What Is the Repair Warranty?

Eligible repairs include a six-month workmanship warranty. Coverage for your specific repair is explained in your service record. Contact us with your repair number if a problem returns.

What Should I Bring?

Bring your device and any charger or accessory connected to the problem. Back up important files if possible. Keep passwords and device passcodes out of online forms.
