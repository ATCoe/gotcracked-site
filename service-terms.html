At Each Step.

An estimate request starts a conversation. A service authorization gives us permission to evaluate a device. Billable repair work requires your approval.

Estimate and Appointment Requests

Submitting an estimate request does not create an appointment. A requested appointment or pickup time is subject to confirmation from the GotCracked team.

Device Evaluation Authorization

When signing the service authorization, you confirm that you own the device or have the owner’s permission to request service. You authorize GotCracked to inspect and diagnose the device described in your request.

We explain the proposed billable work and price for your approval before proceeding. If the proposed work changes, we contact you for approval.

Data and Device Access

Back up important information if possible before handing over your device. Tell us if protecting or recovering data is your priority. Do not submit passwords, unlock codes or payment card details through this website. Discuss any necessary access directly with staff.

Student Pickup and Return

Pickup and return transport is free for student requests accepted by our team. Repair charges are separate. We confirm eligibility, meeting location and timing before collection. Do not leave a device unattended while waiting for pickup.

Your Signed Record

The authorization form records the displayed terms, your name, your signature choice and the time we receive it. You can print or save a copy after submitting. Contact the shop to request a copy later or discuss a correction.
