Customer privacy

Privacy Notice

Effective August 26, 2026. This notice explains how GotCracked uses information submitted through gotcracked.co and during repair service.

Plain-language summary

Your repair information is for your repair.

We use customer information to respond to requests, diagnose and manage repairs, communicate status, arrange shipping, provide receipts, support warranty records, prevent abuse, and operate the business.

🔒

Never send a device password or passcode through this website.
If access is needed for testing, the shop will discuss the safest way to handle it.

Information we collect

Depending on the service you use, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, preferred contact method, shipping or return address, device type and model, repair symptoms, timing constraints, appointment preferences, repair ticket number, chat messages, repair status information, and service or warranty records.

How we use it

We use this information to create and manage repair requests, contact you, schedule service, prepare estimates, document approvals, perform and track repair work, manage parts and shipping, provide receipts, maintain repair and warranty history, respond to questions, prevent spam or abuse, and improve GotCracked operations.

Repair tracking

The public repair tracker requires both a repair ticket number and the phone number associated with the repair. It returns limited repair-status information intended for that customer. Do not share your ticket and phone combination publicly.

Customer chat

Website chat messages are delivered to the GotCracked team so staff can respond. The browser stores a limited chat-session identifier so the conversation can continue when you return from the same device. Avoid sending passwords, payment-card details, or other unnecessary sensitive information in chat.

Service providers

GotCracked uses service providers to operate the website and business systems, including Cloudflare for website delivery, Supabase for application and database services, and Discord for internal staff notifications and customer-chat routing. These providers process information as needed to provide those services.

Information sharing

We do not sell customer information. Information may be shared with service providers that support GotCracked operations, with shipping carriers when needed to deliver a device, or when required by law or necessary to protect customers, GotCracked, or others.

Data retention

Repair, customer, receipt, warranty, and operational records may be retained as reasonably needed for customer service, accounting, warranty support, fraud prevention, legal obligations, and business records. Temporary website or chat data may be retained for a shorter period when it is no longer needed for those purposes.

Security

We use access controls, encrypted web connections, restricted staff systems, and other technical and operational safeguards. No internet-connected system can be guaranteed perfectly secure, so customers should submit only the information needed for service.

Your choices

You may contact GotCracked to ask about correcting customer contact information or to ask a privacy question. Some repair or transaction records may need to be retained for warranty, accounting, fraud-prevention, or legal reasons.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to hello@gotcracked.co or discussed with the GotCracked shop at 700 North Main St, Ste D, Blacksburg, VA 24060.
