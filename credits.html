Image Context

Device photos illustrate repair categories. They are not photographs of customer devices, proof of a GotCracked repair, or listings for devices for sale. We confirm service availability for your exact model.

The blue and steel abstract material background was generated specifically for GotCracked using OpenAI image generation. It depicts no hardware or technical mechanism. Original device photographs remain the factual source for hardware details.

Interface Symbols

Navigation and service controls use the new locally hosted GotCracked interface-symbol library. Symbols are styled to match the repair system and have readable text labels or accessible names where needed.
