Original Artwork. Familiar Devices.

Our visual library combines original GotCracked branding with custom repair illustrations. Device artwork starts from real manufacturer reference images; the finished pieces are illustrations.

Visual library updated October 29, 2026.

Custom Repair Artwork

The cracked glass, repair tools, appointment calendar, protective shield, learning guide, and custom-PC compositions were generated specifically for GotCracked using OpenAI image generation. Transparent backgrounds let the foreground artwork share the site's lighting and page surfaces.

Device Image References

Device illustrations use real Apple and Sony images as visual baselines for recognizable proportions and component layouts:

  • Apple iPhone 16 Pro exterior manufacturer image
  • Apple iPad Air 11-inch M2 internal view
  • Apple MacBook Air M2 2022 logic board repair reference
  • Sony PlayStation 5 Pro teardown motherboard photograph

These are service-category illustrations, not photographs of customer devices, proof of completed GotCracked repairs, technical repair instructions, or listings for devices for sale. We confirm service availability for your exact device.

Interface Symbols

Navigation and service controls use the new locally hosted GotCracked interface-symbol library. Symbols are styled to match the repair system and have readable text labels or accessible names where needed.
