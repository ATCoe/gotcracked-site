Built for GotCracked

Repair is the visual language.

The site’s blue, black, and metallic visual system follows the original GotCracked wordmark and lightning mark. The artwork explains repair categories and technical ideas; it does not depict a real customer repair or a photographed GotCracked workbench.

Visual library updated October 28, 2026.

01

GotCracked repair artwork

The current repair illustrations were generated specifically for GotCracked’s customer website. They are illustrative technical concepts, not manufacturer photographs, customer-device records, diagnostic evidence, or images of the shop.

Illustrations can depict exploded assemblies, damaged components, test equipment, and repair systems. Their visual details do not guarantee a particular repair procedure, part, service, or outcome for your device.

02

Image context

The customer-facing repair artwork replaces the earlier manufacturer product photographs, stock references, and retired generated illustration sets. A device or component shown in concept artwork is representative. We confirm service availability and repair requirements for the exact device you bring in.

The original GotCracked logo remains the brand identity. Official social-platform marks may appear only as links to configured GotCracked profiles.

03

Interface symbols

Navigation and service controls use the new locally hosted GotCracked interface-symbol library. Symbols are styled to match the repair system and have readable text labels or accessible names where needed.
